Our Legacy

Homestyle Cooking With Italian Heart

Family-owned and operated in Cleveland, NC, Mario's is a cornerstone of the local community. We pride ourselves on serving generous portions of homemade Italian specialties, classic Philly cheesesteaks, Greek gyros and souvlaki, and homestyle comfort dinners. Whether you're dining in our cozy dining room or ordering takeout for the family, you're treated like one of our own.
