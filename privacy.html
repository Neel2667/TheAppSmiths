Privacy Policy for SafePass

Effective Date: September 27, 2025
App Name: SafePass — Offline Password Manager

1. Introduction

SafePass is a secure password manager that prioritizes your privacy and security. Your sensitive information stays on your device and is protected with strong encryption. SafePass offers both free and premium versions to meet different user needs. SafePass is developed and maintained by The Appsmiths. We believe in data ownership: you are always in control of your data.

2. Information We Collect

SafePass collects minimal data to provide our service:

  • Passwords and account information: Stored locally on your device only
  • App usage data: Basic analytics for app improvement (anonymous)
  • Ad data (Free users only): Standard advertising identifiers for ad serving
  • No personal information, location data, or browsing history is collected

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your passwords, notes, and vault data are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM encryption. A master password (chosen by you) derives the encryption key using a secure algorithm (PBKDF2 or Argon2). Biometric unlock (Face ID / Fingerprint) uses the official Android BiometricPrompt API and does not store or transmit biometric data.

4. Backups & Export

By default, SafePass does not create backups. You may optionally export an encrypted backup file or QR code. Backups remain under your control and are not uploaded unless you choose to save them to a location (e.g., SD card, personal cloud). If stored externally, you are responsible for keeping your backup safe.

5. Permissions Used

SafePass requests minimal permissions:

  • Biometric authentication: to unlock your vault using Face ID or fingerprint.
  • Storage access (optional): only if you choose to import/export encrypted backup files.
  • No other permissions are requested.

6. Third-Party Services

SafePass integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Free users only): For serving advertisements
  • Google Play Services: For in-app purchases
  • Premium users have completely ad-free experience with no advertising data collection

7. Children's Privacy

SafePass is designed for general use and is rated "Everyone" on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Freemium Model

SafePass offers two versions:

  • Free Version: Store up to 5 passwords with non-intrusive ads
  • Premium Version: Unlimited passwords, ad-free experience ($4.99 one-time purchase)
  • Premium users have completely ad-free experience with no advertising data collection

9. Your Rights & Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device: You can add, edit, and delete your data anytime. Uninstalling the app will permanently erase your vault unless you created a manual backup.

10. Security Measures

All vault data is protected using AES-256 encryption. Auto-lock, clipboard auto-clear, and screenshot blocking are implemented for extra security. Your master password is never stored by SafePass.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be available in the app and on our website.
