Privacy Policy for SafePass

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SafePass ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. SafePass was built from the ground up with absolute privacy and security in mind. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

1. Data Collection and Storage (100% Offline)

SafePass is a fully offline, zero-knowledge password manager. The App does not require internet access, nor does it connect to any remote servers.

  • Your Vault Data: All passwords, notes, and metadata you enter are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Local Storage Only: Your encrypted data is stored exclusively on your local device's secure storage. We have absolutely no access to your vault or master password.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To provide its core functionality, SafePass may request the following device permissions:

  • Biometrics / Fingerprint: Used locally via the Android BiometricPrompt API to securely unlock your vault. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is processed securely by the Android OS.
  • Storage / File Access: Used strictly for the Backup & Restore feature, allowing you to export or import your encrypted vault locally to your device storage.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

SafePass does not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks. Your usage remains completely private and anonymous.

4. Data Security

We utilize the Android Keystore system to securely generate and store the encryption keys used to protect your vault. Because the App is zero-knowledge, if you lose your Master PIN/Password, we cannot recover your data for you.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, as we do not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
