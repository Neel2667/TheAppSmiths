Privacy Policy for SafePass

Effective Date: September 27, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to the SafePass mobile application (the "App") developed by The AppSmiths.

1. Data Collection and Usage

SafePass is an offline application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data, including your master password or any information you store within the app (such as usernames, passwords, notes, etc.). All your data is stored locally and encrypted on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

2. Permissions

The SafePass app does not require any special permissions to function, such as access to your contacts, location, or network. The app only requires access to your device's local storage to save your encrypted data file.

3. Data Security

We take your security seriously. All data you enter into SafePass is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption before it is saved to your device. Your Master Password is the key to decrypt this data and is never stored by the app. It is your responsibility to keep your Master Password safe.

4. Children's Privacy

SafePass does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app operates entirely offline, it is impossible for us to collect such data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app and on our website. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us
